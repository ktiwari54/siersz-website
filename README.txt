Siersz website (static)

Chinese (main) pages are in this folder; English pages are in en/.
Every [bracketed] item shown with a yellow background is a placeholder.
Edit the settings at the top of site.py (company name, email, phone, address) and re-run: python site.py
Open index.html in a browser to preview. To publish, upload the whole folder to the web host for siersz.com.
The yellow "placeholder" bar at the top of each page is controlled by the "draft" text in site.py; remove it before launch.
Legal pages are drafts: have them reviewed by a lawyer before launch.
Photos in images/photos come from Unsplash (free licence, see unsplash.com/license). Replace them with your own photos when you have them.
The iPhone photos show real Apple devices and logos (stock photos). State in the Terms of Use that you are an independent reseller with no Apple affiliation, and replace them with photos of your own stock when available.
