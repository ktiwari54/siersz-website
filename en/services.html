What We Do

Wholesale supply of pre-owned Apple devices — sourced, graded and delivered.

Wholesale supply

We supply pre-owned iPhones and Apple products in bulk to business buyers. Orders can be a single model and grade, or a mixed assortment — we work to your specification. Pricing is volume-based and confirmed upfront.

Device grading and inspection

Every device is inspected and graded before it leaves us. We use a consistent A / B / C scale — Grade A is near-mint, Grade B has light cosmetic marks, Grade C has visible wear but passes all function tests. Battery health is checked and recorded. Data is fully erased.

Fulfilment and shipping

Orders are dispatched within 24–48 hours of payment. We arrange logistics and provide tracking. For large or regular orders, we can discuss dedicated shipping arrangements.

After-order support

We stay available after delivery. If there is a grading discrepancy or a question about your order, contact us within 48 hours and we will resolve it.
